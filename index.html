A Fractional AI Lead works with your business part-time and flexibly — one, two, or four sessions a month, based on what you need. You don't hire a full-time executive or carry that cost. You get their strategic judgment, technical criteria, and network exactly when you need them, at a fraction of the usual cost.A Fractional AI Lead részmunkaidőben és rugalmasan dolgozik a vállalkozásoddal — havi egy, két vagy négy alkalommal, az igényeid szerint.
